LAUNCHING AT GIS MELA · 9:45 A.M.
CWR³Data
The flood data and modeling platform from FAU's Center for Water Resiliency and Risk Reduction (CWR³), launched live on stage.
Data Exchange  PyWMP-WEB  AeroFuse  Notebooks
Find flood maps, elevation and land cover, run flood models, process drone imagery and USGS LiDAR, and ask CWR³ questions in plain English, all in one place.
Open beta: anyone can sign up, no FAU email needed.
