          03

          CWR³, Florida Atlantic University · 2023 – present

          Flood Modelling & Watershed Planning

          Automated workflows and an open Python platform for Florida flood studies.

          Watershed master plans need many basins and scenarios, processed consistently from LiDAR terrain. I lead the geospatial work at CWR³ for communities including:

          HialeahHomesteadNorth Miami Miami-Dade CountyBroward CountyPolk CountySanta Rosa County

          Automated workflow: 48 scenarios per region with 85% less manual work (lead author, Natural Hazards). PyWMP: a Python platform combining HEC-HMS methods and Cascade 2001 routing, with 1D, 2D and hybrid models on CPU or GPU.

                  05

                  CWR³Data · 2025 – 2026

                  CWR³Data Platform

                  A data portal and modelling workbench for watershed research.

                  Watershed studies need elevation, rainfall, soil and gauge data from many sources, and results that are easy to share. CWR³Data brings data and models into one browser workspace, with 28 built-in tutorials.

                  • Data CatalogSearch, preview and request Florida water data, with standard metadata.
                  • PyWMP-WEB1D, 2D and hybrid flood models in the browser, running on the GPU.
                  • AeroFuseDrone imagery and 3DEP LiDAR turned into terrain models, orthophotos and change maps.
                  • NotebooksGeoAI on the GPU, next to the data.

                  Public beta · launching

                  CWR³Data goes public at GIS Mela, November 17, 2026

                  GIS Mela is World GIS Day at FAU, a one-day festival of maps, data and the people who make them. Details, RSVP and sponsorship are on the event page.

                    2026 – present

                    Current Research

                    Hydrologic AI, climate–vegetation analysis and open flood modelling.

                    Hydrologic AI (ESTGCN): graph neural networks that use watershed topology to predict runoff; they outperform distance-based graphs.

                    Climate–vegetation trends: 43 years of NDVI across Florida, separating climate trends from hurricanes and drought.

                    Open flood modelling (PyWMP): one framework for 1D, 2D and hybrid event-based models.

                    All three manuscripts are under review (Publications, code). Open to research, industry and collaboration opportunities: get in touch.
